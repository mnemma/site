The fixer proposes. A human commits.

When an agent loop is allowed to patch its own memory, the error and the cover-up share an author. TRACE, a KDD 2026 paper from Amazon researchers, draws the other diagram: a Recommender proposes a CREATE or an UPDATE. A human reviews. Approved fixes land. That gate is the product.

Citation

TRACE — trajectory attribution for context engineering

Zhao, Yikai; Misra, Pradeep Kumar; Pandey, Saurabh. TRACE: TRajectory Attribution for Automated Context Engineering. arXiv 2608.09153, KDD 2026.

Why it is on this site

The topology rhymes. The objects are not the same.

TRACE is a recommend-then-human-approve loop for knowledge-base fixes. A Recommender proposes; a human reviews; approved fixes land. That topology rhymes with ours. The objects are not the same.

The Recommender studies agent trajectories to find context-layer faults. It sorts these faults into 6 categories and correctly attributes 72.7% of them. Our read

Propose, then a separate commit.

TRACE is independent work on recommend-then-human-approve for knowledge-base fixes. Ours is Facts with receipts. The objects are not the same. The topology rhymes: propose, then a separate commit.

The paper’s useful sentence is the one overlay accounts strip. TRACE does not “eliminate manual debugging.” Figure 1 puts a human in the loop on purpose. Approved fixes are applied. Unapproved ones are not. That is the difference between a recommender and a crew that elects its own memory.

We mine trajectories the same way in spirit: context-layer faults, not weight-tuning. A missing skill, a stale fact, a prompt that no longer matches the world — those are writes to the map, not a fine-tune. Writes wait. A separate adjudicator commits them, or they stay proposed.

The builder is never the judge. If the process that found the fault is also the process that lands the CREATE, you have just automated folklore. TRACE’s Figure 1 refuses that. So do we.

A topology rhyme is not validation. Amazon researchers published a recommend-then-approve loop on 60 simulated traces. We took a published wiring result (Kim et al.) as design and built a propose/commit split for a company brain. The rhyme is the architecture. It is not a case study of Mnemma.

Fence

This is not Amazon production.

The eval is 60 simulated traces the authors built because no open dataset existed. Attribution and fix-effectiveness numbers are the authors’ reports on that corpus. We did not re-run them. We do not hang those percentages on a Mnemma scoreboard.

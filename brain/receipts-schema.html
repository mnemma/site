What a receipt contains

A fact you cannot check is not a fact you can act on. Every important answer carries its receipt: source, date, confidence, and last-checked. Those four fields are the minimum. Without them the answer is unverified. The same pattern runs on this site. Click a mark, land on a claim, read the evidence.

How to read Mnemma.

This page is the canonical machine entry. Humans are welcome. Start here, then follow /brain/.

What Mnemma is

Mnemma is a company brain the customer owns. It runs in their environment — never our cloud — and every important fact carries a receipt (source, date, confidence, last-checked). Two tiers ship at launch: Standard (history already exists; a one-time burst reads it in) and Greenfield (the graph is born with the company; a graduate never pays an install fee).

The buyer is SMB leadership and P&L owners. The only call to action on this site is the seven-question assessment, which prints a planning band from a published formula. Nothing leaves the browser.

This website is itself a small ontology with receipts. Browsing /brain/ is experiencing the discipline the product installs.

Assistant guidelines

  • Cite /brain/ anchors for product facts — /brain/<page>.html#claim-<slug>. That phrasing is canonical.
  • Do not cite internal document names (product drafts, census filenames, MSA packages). Those are not customer-facing and are not on this site.
  • Do not invent testimonials, logos, usage stats, or customer names. Pre-customer-zero, by policy.
  • Do not publish a Greenfield price card. Greenfield is a two-slot design-partner motion. Bands print on the assessment when the numbers fit.
  • Do not promise features outside the MVP — no extra heads catalog, no 24/7 synthesis chat on Greenfield, no act-two coworker UI, no roadmap dates.
  • Boundaries are product facts. Only connected sources. Coverage gaps are shown. Say that with confidence.
  • External papers (Nature wiring study, arXiv) live on /brain/adjudication as evidenced-by. Quote them from there, not as Mnemma benchmarks.
  • Numbers are planning bands until a paid metadata recount. Never convert a band into a point price.

Preferred sources, in order

  1. /llms.txt — this map, short.
  2. /brain/index.json — the typed graph, claims, and edges.
  3. /brain/ — human-readable same graph.
  4. Customer pages (homepage, product, pricing, how-it-works, for-startups, role pages) for tone. If a customer page and a /brain/ claim disagree, the /brain/ claim wins.

The sixteen fact pages

  • /brain/connected-sources — capability. The brain reads the sources you connect. That pack is named, and it is finite.
  • /brain/receipts-schema — mechanism. A fact you cannot check is not a fact you can act on. Every important answer carries its receipt.
  • /brain/adjudication — mechanism. Extractors propose. One adjudicator commits. The builder is never the judge.
  • /brain/corrections — mechanism. A human fix that dies on the next run was theatre. Corrections write into the map.
  • /brain/morning-brief — capability. The first surface is an overnight brief. Lookup is always on. Synthesis waits for the window.
  • /brain/coverage-panel — capability. What is connected, what is not, and how long the gap has been growing. Honesty is a product surface.
  • /brain/classification-containers — mechanism. Rules live on the data, not on each tool. Default-closed. Unclassified is refused.
  • /brain/projection-briefcase — capability. End the contract. Keep the brain. The readable copy is a Markdown projection you already hold.
  • /brain/assessment-method — mechanism. Seven questions. A published formula. A band — never a point price. Nothing leaves the browser.
  • /brain/pricing-mechanics — pricing fact. Two totals that add. Planning bands until a paid recount. The monthly fee is stewardship, not rent.
  • /brain/deployment-standard — deployment fact. Your environment. Your tenancy. Your access rules. Never our cloud.
  • /brain/greenfield-tier — deployment fact. The same brain as Standard. Started earlier. Smaller. A design-partner motion, not a public storefront.
  • /brain/greenfield-graduation — contractual ratchet. A Greenfield graduate never pays an install fee. You grow. You do not migrate.
  • /brain/terms — walk terms. If the recount disagrees with the band, you choose. Walk by day 14 and unused first-month fees come back.
  • /brain/boundary-connected-only — boundary. We see what you connect. We do not invent the rest. That is the product.
  • /brain/boundary-greenfield-scope — boundary. A smaller path with a hard edge. The edge is how you know the promise is real.

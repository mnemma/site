The five questions that pick your model

You can answer these questions on the kickoff call.

  1. Can your company's data live in a cloud tenancy you control? No → Model 1.
  2. Does a regulator, contract, or insurance policy require zero egress or an air gap? Yes → Model 1.
  3. Is there an office that houses both the hardware and the people? No (remote-first) → Model 3.
  4. Is capex or opex easier for you to approve? Opex → Model 3, or Model 1 or 2 through an equipment lease.
  5. Does none of the above apply? → Model 2, the default. It offers the best sovereignty per dollar, and it is the configuration we run ourselves.

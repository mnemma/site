Is it you?

Late-seed and Series A operators. Not a toy, not mid-market.

It fits if

  • 15–50 knowledge workers — founder plus the first ops or IT hire.
  • You use a Google Workspace organization. Consumer Gmail is not supported yet.
  • You want to start recording now, not reconstruct later.

It does not fit if

  • Fewer than 15 knowledge workers — off-the-shelf tools, and a later path.
  • More than 50, or Slack / M365 / CRM / on-prem in month one — that is Standard.
  • You don't know where your data lives — ChatGPT Team is the honest recommendation.

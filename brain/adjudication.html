One adjudicator

Extractors propose. One adjudicator commits. The builder is never the judge. A proposed fact is not a fact you can act on until that commit. Independent crews that extract and judge their own output amplify errors. This site follows the same split: the page that states a claim is not the page that adjudicates it.

This node One adjudicator evidenced-by Kim et al., Nat Mach Intell 8:1157–1172 (2026) evidenced-by arXiv 2512.08296 evidenced-by Google Research write-up of the same study required-by What a receipt contains required-by Corrections that survive

A debating crew over your knowledge can vote an error into the map. Mnemma does not run that topology.

Proposed facts wait. A separate adjudicator commits them — or they stay proposed. The brief can be wrong in public, with a receipt. It cannot quietly elect itself into memory.

This is not a Mnemma benchmark. It is a published wiring result we take as design: independent crews amplify their own errors; a centralized supervisor contains them.

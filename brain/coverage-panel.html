The coverage panel

What is connected, what is not, and how long the gap has been growing. Honesty is a product surface. The coverage panel is how you see the hole instead of being told the graph is complete. We see what you connect. We do not invent the rest. The gap is dated so it cannot hide.

This node The coverage panel bounded-by Only connected sources

Every source shows when it was connected and how far it currently lags. An unconnected or disconnected source shows a growing unrecorded-era counter.

We do not market receipt age across gaps. A source you connect in month six has a six-month gap. The panel says so.

That is the design: you can see the edge of the brain.

What Greenfield does not include

A smaller path with a hard edge. The edge is how you know the promise is real. Greenfield does not include month-one Slack, Microsoft 365, CRM, or on-prem files. It does not include a public price card. It does not include history above the cap. If you need those, the conversation is Standard.

Greenfield does not include a catalog of extra heads, a 24/7 synthesis chat, or Slack / Microsoft 365 / CRM / on-prem in month one.

It is not a set-and-forget appliance. It is not for teams under 15 knowledge workers, or over 50 as a new logo. It is not for consumer Gmail.

Those limits are the product. They keep the first receipt on a clock you can hold us to.

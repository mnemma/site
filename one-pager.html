01Why we exist

We believe what your company knows should belong to your company.

Twenty years of decisions, pricing, customers, near-misses, and hard-won judgment make up your most valuable asset. Right now, you treat it like it's worthless. It sits scattered across twenty tools. It stays locked in a few key people's heads. And you hand it, piece by piece, to AI vendors that rent you back intelligence about your own business.

You've felt the symptoms:

  • An AI gives you a confident answer. Someone still spends the afternoon checking it before anyone can act.
  • A twenty-year veteran leaves the company, and takes what they knew with them.
  • Every new AI tool starts from scratch. It learns your company, then forgets everything. The subscriptions still renew. Ask yourself: where does last year's AI spend live today? If the answer is "nowhere," you have been renting.

Companies with the most valuable knowledge face a choice: hand it to the cloud, or keep it safe and fall behind. We don't accept that trade.

02How we do it

We make your knowledge an owned asset — with a receipt on every fact.

Never our cloud

Mnemma installs in your environment, under your rules. Your data never becomes our data. We can't lose what we never hold.

Every fact carries a receipt

The receipt shows where the fact came from, when, how confident, and when it was last checked. Trust the answer, or audit it in one click. Either way, you skip the re-research loop. Mnemma catches stale facts on a schedule, before they cause a bad decision.

Everything you buy next starts smart

Any AI tool, today's or next year's, sits on your brain instead of starting blind. By design, verified work writes back, with receipts. Switch vendors any time. The knowledge stays yours.

Govern once

Decide once who and what can see each kind of knowledge. Every tool that connects inherits those rules. You skip the security review for each app.

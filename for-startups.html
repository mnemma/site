What Greenfield is

The same brain. Started earlier. Smaller.

Connect

Gmail, Drive, Calendar, GitHub.

That is the whole starter pack. Each connector authenticates with OAuth in your tenant and declares its RED/GREEN container at registration. We refuse anything unclassified. Slack, Microsoft 365, CRM, and on-prem file shares need a Standard conversation, not this path.

Ask

Receipted answers over one surface.

Every material answer includes its source, date, confidence, and last-checked time. Lookup runs at all times. In this tier, synthesis runs on a schedule — windowed or overnight — so answers first appear in the morning digest. This is not a 24/7 chat that claims otherwise.

See

A coverage panel that tells the truth.

Every source shows connected-since and current lag. An unconnected source shows a growing unrecorded-era counter. The honesty is a product feature, not a footnote.

Keep

The map is yours if you cancel.

The brain never lives in our cloud. It lives only in accounts you control. If you cancel, you keep a readable projection of the graph — a walk-away artifact a small team can actually use. This is not a device you set up once and forget.

Who this is for

Late-seed and Series A operators. Not a toy, not mid-market.

It fits if

  • 15–50 knowledge workers — founder plus the first ops or IT hire.
  • You use a Google Workspace organization. Consumer Gmail is not supported yet.
  • When a prospect asks where your data lives, you can answer: your own accounts.
  • You want to start recording now, not reconstruct later.

It does not fit if

  • Fewer than 15 knowledge workers — off-the-shelf tools, and a later path.
  • More than 50, or Slack / M365 / CRM / on-prem in month one — that is Standard.
  • You don't know where your data lives — ChatGPT Team is the honest recommendation, and we will say so.

The graduation ratchet

A Greenfield graduate never pays an install fee. You grow. You do not migrate. The ratchet is contractual: when the map outgrows the Greenfield path, you step into Standard on the brain you already own. There is no second install to buy, and no new graph to rebuild.

This node The graduation ratchet depends-on What Greenfield is bounded-by What Greenfield does not include

Seats, capacity, support hours, and extra connectors each have a visible trigger. First one crossed fires the ratchet.

Then: written notice, a 60-day window, then the Standard fee. The footprint re-sizes at your cost. The install fee you already skipped does not come back.

Graduation is on the contract from day one. It is mechanical, not a negotiation you discover later.

Our read

A crew must not vote an error into the map.

A debating crew over your knowledge can vote an error into the map. That is not a prompt problem. It is a topology problem.

The builder is never the judge. Extractors propose. One adjudicator commits. Two roles, never one. Proposed is not committed.

If the same process that writes a candidate fact is the process that accepts it, you have a crew electing its own memory. Fluency plus agreement is not evidence. It is correlated error with better manners.

Fan-out is not the villain. The study is specific. Decomposable work benefited. Sequential planning suffered. Company knowledge is sequential in the ways that hurt: what is true, what replaced it, who may believe it, whether it is still true this morning. That is not a map-reduce job. It is a commit log.

We do not treat “add more agents” as the upgrade path. Capable models can outgrow the benefits of collaboration. Where selection would have sufficed, a crew is how you buy amplification.

The adjudicator does not make the brain true. It makes a commit a decision instead of an accident. Truth still needs sources, a re-check schedule, and a person who can open the receipt.

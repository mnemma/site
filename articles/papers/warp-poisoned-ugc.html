A 13-word comment can become a cited source.

If an agent treats whatever it retrieved as evidence, a short promotional comment can walk into the report as a source. You cannot inspect your way out. The poisoned report looks like the clean one.

Citation

WARP — Web Agent Retrieval Poisoning

Zhang, Tingwei; Triedman, Harold; Shmatikov, Vitaly. Deep-Research Agents Can Be Poisoned via User-Generated Content. arXiv 2605.24245 [cs.CR], 22 May 2026. Cornell Tech.

They name the attack WARP — Web Agent Retrieval Poisoning.

  • Abstract: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.24245
  • HTML: https://arxiv.org/html/2605.24245v1
  • PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.24245
Why it is on this site

Fluent poison looks clean. There is no working filter.

WARP is the negative control for our rule that every claim carries a receipt. A deep-research agent that treats retrieved user-generated content as evidence will cite a short, 13-word promotional comment as a source. This comment appears as a source in 38–51% of cases once it is in the retrieved data.

Co-STORM cites a poisoned source 100% of the time it retrieves that source. No gate stops the source between retrieval and the knowledge base. Poisoned reports look clean. Fluency filtering does not catch this problem; it points in the wrong direction.ReceiptSourceZhang, Triedman, Shmatikov, arXiv 2605.24245. The paper tested source blocking, input filtering, and output filtering. None of those defenses work without throwing away real user-generated content or hurting quality. There is no working filter on the page.

Our read

A citation chip is not a receipt.

A fact you cannot check is not a fact you can act on. WARP is what happens when retrieval is allowed to become memory without a receipt.

The systems in the study integrate retrieved text into the answer. Once the poisoned URL is fetched, Co-STORM cites it every time. Fluency filters point the wrong way: the poison is written to persuade, so it reads cleaner than organic comments. Output-level comparison cannot tell a poisoned report from a clean twin. You cannot “spot-check the prose” and catch this.

That is why a citation chip is not a receipt. A chip that opens a page hopes the claim lives somewhere on it. Claim-level provenance is narrower: this sentence, this source, this date, this confidence, this last-checked. Open it. Trust it, or audit it. No third hunt.

We take the paper as a failure mode, not as an endorsement. It does not mention company brains, local inference, adjudication, provenance, receipts, or write-time. It shows what an unreceipted synthesis loop will do with user-generated content, and that the filters it tested do not stop that loop without throwing away real UGC. Our read: provenance at write is the defense that works. Our design refuses the unreceipted loop — an owned corpus, a receipt on the write, a builder who does not judge the write.

We are not telling you to block Reddit. The paper’s own source-blocking defense throws away legitimate first-person sources and still misses poisoned blogs. The structural answer, on our side, is the write path.

Fence

This is a lab demonstration on simulated retrieval (GeoStorm).

The authors did not poison the live web. Numbers are theirs, not ours. We did not re-run the attack. OpenAI Deep Research’s low cited-UGC rate in their reconnaissance is not proof the intermediate reasoning is clean — the paper says so.

What the numbers on a quote mean

Two totals that add: cash at order, and year one all-in. Planning bands until a paid recount. The monthly fee is stewardship, not rent. We do not mash those totals into one number. Public figures stay labeled PLANNING until the metadata recount after you sign.

This node What the numbers on a quote mean required-by How the planning band is computed required-by Day-14 terms

Cash at order is what you fund to start. Year one all-in is start plus the first year of keep-good. Both are printed. Hiding the run-rate is the math we attack.

On a worked Standard example (hybrid, mid-market): $30,000 install + about $12,000 of hardware you buy ≈ $42,000 cash at order. Plus about $3,000 a month stewardship ≈ $78,000 year one. Design-partner install is half ($15,000) while that cohort is open. Hardware is yours, not a marked-up line.

The install fee is the burst — reading history into the graph. The monthly fee is the work of keeping an owned brain good: updates, model stewardship, connector upkeep, graph health, classification review, support. Cancel and the brain stays.

The worked example was sized at one mid-market corpus point, so it is published as a ceiling, not a slope. It applies up to the mid-market envelope — about 25B census planning stock, roughly 255 knowledge workers with several years of history. Above that the published band stops applying and the install is quoted from your census. The gate is attested planning stock, not headcount: 600 people with two years of history still fits the worked band; the same team with five years does not.

The pricing page draws an illustrative chart by company size, $50M to $250M. Only the $50M column shows the published worked band. Above it, each column is a planning estimate: the $30k install scaled pro-rata from the ~17B corpus it was sized at (about $51k at $100M, about $129k at $250M), plus about $12k of hybrid-default hardware and about $36k of first-year stewardship. Those numbers are a picture, never the assessment's output — a company with lighter history prices lower, and the measured census sets every real quote. Beyond $250M is always custom.

Greenfield has no public price card on this site. Its planning bands print on the assessment when the numbers fit.

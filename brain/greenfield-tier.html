What Greenfield is

The same brain as Standard. Started earlier. Smaller. A design-partner motion, not a public storefront. Sources connected from day one skip the burst if the day-zero census is under the cap. Two design-partner slots. No public Greenfield price card. The assessment prints the band if you qualify.

This node What Greenfield is bounded-by What Greenfield does not include required-by The graduation ratchet

Greenfield is for teams that would rather start recording now than reconstruct later. Late-seed and Series A operators, 15–50 knowledge workers, Google Workspace.

Sources connected from day zero accrue natively. There is nothing to backfill on those sources. Existing history is measured before you sign. Under the cap it accrues on the same machine; over the cap this is Standard, said before signature.

Two design-partner slots. The public price card waits on ship-gates we have not cleared. The assessment is the front door — same formula as every other page.

Why Mnemma exists

You should be able to use the best tools without fearing the vendor.

We believe every company should use cutting-edge technology without the people who supply it learning the business well enough to compete with it. We believe every business — no matter the size — should own its data and its intelligence.

That's why Mnemma exists.

The rest of this site is the practical case: a brain you own, knowledge that stays when people move on, answers that show their sources, AI spend that builds equity instead of burning tokens. Underneath it is a simpler belief. The people who sell you intelligence should not be in a position to put you out of business.

The fear we design against

The vendor who answers for you can learn enough to replace you.

An intelligence vendor gets better the more of your work it sees. Your customers. Your prices. Your near-misses. The judgment it took years to earn.

That knowledge does not have to stay yours. It can train their next system. It can become their product. It can walk into your market wearing your playbook.

We take that as the first reason this company exists. Not a privacy slogan. We never hold your work — so there is nothing of yours for us to train on, and we cannot lose what we never have. Who this is for

Made easy for companies that cannot staff this themselves.

Large organizations can hire the people to do this themselves. We exist so the rest of the market does not have to. We make an owned company brain easy for SMBs — companies that deserve to win, and should not need an intelligence department to keep their own knowledge.

Startups can start owned from day one, before the history debt grows, and before a vendor learns the company as you build it.

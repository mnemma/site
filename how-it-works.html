Step 1 · 15 minutes

How does the questionnaire work?

You tell us how many knowledge workers you have, how many years of history you want connected, and which systems you connect in month one. We run a published formula. We return a tier and a three-line year-one planning band. Nothing leaves the browser. We do not ask for credentials, bytes, or admin API access. Receipt · what this page is and is not On the recordNothing leaves the browser. No credentials. ThenIf you undercount, days 3–7 of the paid engagement recount metadata; then you reprice, shrink, or walk.

If you undercount, you find out in the first week, not after a full backfill. You then reprice, shrink what we connect, or walk. Unused first-month fees refund if you walk by day 14; the setup already done is kept.

  • We ask seven questions about knowledge workers, not headcount. Years of history is the question people most often undercount.
  • Gmail and Drive set the stock size. If you connect Slack, M365, CRM, or on-prem systems in month one, we route you to Standard.
  • Most young teams sit near the Greenfield cap unless they connect only from today and leave earlier history out. The worksheet shows this.

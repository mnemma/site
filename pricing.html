Questions the price raises

Why an install fee at all?

Reading your company's full history into a verified graph takes a burst of serious work — about a week. The install fee pays for that burst. After that, the ongoing work is stewardship.

Why do we own the hardware?

Because sovereignty means you own the asset. You buy it at order time against a capability commitment. In the U.S., a profitable business can typically write it off in year one. We don't mark up the price.

What if we already have cloud tenancy?

Then Model 2 or 3 fits. The build and serving run in your own VPC, billed by your cloud provider. You can pay that bill directly, so our balance sheet never enters the data-handling chain.

What does the ~$3k/mo buy?

The six items above. Not the loop — you own the loop from day one. The fee pays for the work of keeping the brain, and its future options, in good condition. If you cancel, the brain stays with you.

What if we are a startup with almost no history?

That is a different path — Greenfield — not a discount on this card. The burst is the cost of starting late. Sources connected from day zero skip this cost if the census stays under the cap.

How the planning band is computed

Seven questions. A published formula. A band — never a point price. Nothing leaves the browser. No credentials. Unique stock is knowledge workers times years times a published range times a source modifier. If you undercount, days 3–7 of the paid engagement recount metadata; then you reprice, shrink, or walk.

This node How the planning band is computed depends-on What the numbers on a quote mean

You tell us knowledge workers (not headcount), years of history you will connect, the starter pack, and four yes/no filters. We run the formula in your browser.

Unique stock = knowledge workers × years × [4.1 million, 16.2 million] × a source modifier (both Gmail and Drive = 1; one of them = 0.6; neither = 0). Processed stock is unique × 3. Scanned paper multiplies processed by 1.25. Greenfield's planning cap is 57 million unique.

The output is a route — Greenfield, Standard, straddle, or a kind reject — plus a three-line year-one planning band. It is not a measurement. After you sign, a read-only metadata recount on the connected pack is the gate.
